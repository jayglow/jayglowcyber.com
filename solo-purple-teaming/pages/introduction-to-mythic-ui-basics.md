## **Introduction**

In this lecture, we will walk through some key features of the **Mythic C2 framework UI**. While this is **not** a complete course on Mythic or the UI, you need a basic understanding of how to navigate it so you can effectively solo purple team using the framework.

We’ll explore the **main dashboard** and essential icons, giving you the foundation to interact with agents, manage callbacks, and prepare for future operations.

---

### **1. Accessing the Mythic Dashboard**

- The **Mythic Dashboard** is your **command center**.

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image.png)

- From here, you can:
    - Monitor **callbacks** (active agent connections)
    - Manage **implants**
    - Interact with **target systems**
- The interface is **clean**, **modular**, and **scales well** for larger red team operations.
- **Dashboard Elements** include:
    - **Task Status** – tracks running and completed tasks.
    - **Number of Callbacks** – shows active sessions with compromised hosts.
    - **Top Command Stats** – displays the most frequently executed commands.

---

### **2. Profiles and Payload Types**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%201.png)

- Click the **Profiles & Payload Types** icon to view:
    - **Agents Installed** – e.g., *Apollo Agent*
    - **C2 Profiles** – such as HTTP and SMB
    - **Service Wrappers** – additional operational components.

---

### **3. Search Function**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%202.png)

- The **Search** icon allows you to:
    - Look up specific information within your operation
    - Search by agent, callback, or collected indicators.

---

### **4. Files Section**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%203.png)

- Contains **all transferred files** in the operation:
    - **Downloads** – view files pulled from targets.
    - **Uploads** – review files sent to targets.
    - **File Browser** – navigate retrieved directories.
    - **Screenshots** – view images captured from the target system.
- Some **event workflow tools** are also accessible here (covered in later lectures).

---

### **5. Artifacts**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%204.png)

- Any artifact generated by a task (e.g., file creation, process execution) is stored here.
- Useful for **post-operation review** and **forensics**.

---

### **6. Proxies**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%205.png)

- Displays **SOCKS proxies** or tunneling configurations in use.
- Essential for pivoting into other network segments.

---

### **7. Screenshots**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%206.png)

- Stores all **captured screenshots** from compromised hosts.

---

### **8. Credentials**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%207.png)

- Central location for all **collected credentials**.
- Supports sorting and filtering for quick access.

---

### **9. Active Callbacks**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%208.png)

- **One of the most important icons.**
- Shows **live agent connections**.
- When an **assumed breach host** (e.g., in the *ecoin domain*) calls back, you’ll see it here.
- Clicking a callback lets you:
    - **Interact** with the compromised host.
    - **Issue tasks** directly to the agent.

---

### **10. Operations**

![image.png](Introduction%20to%20Mythic%20UI%20Basics/image%209.png)

- Displays the current **operation name** (e.g., *Test Apollo Reflection*).
- Clicking it shows:
    - **Command lists** for the operation.
    - Options to **create new operations** (used in future lectures for *Level Zero Attack Path*).

---

### **Action Before Moving On**

- Spend a few minutes exploring the Mythic UI.
- Check:
    - Which **listeners** are installed.
    - Which **payload types** are available.
- Familiarize yourself with each main icon so you can quickly navigate during live operations.
