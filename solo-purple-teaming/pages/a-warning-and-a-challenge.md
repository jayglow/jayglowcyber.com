## **1. Understanding the Shelf Life of Evasion Techniques**

- **Key Point:** Evasion techniques (such as AMSI bypasses) do not work forever.
- **Reason:** Security tools like antivirus, EDR, and AMSI itself are constantly updated.
- **Impact:** A bypass that is effective today may be patched or signatured tomorrow.
- **Takeaway:** Success in this field requires adaptability—constantly learning and modifying techniques as the detection landscape changes.

---

### **2. Importance of Having a Working AMSI Bypass**

- **Requirement:** Before you proceed to the next course stage, you **must** have a functional AMSI bypass.
- **No Pre-Built Solution Provided:**
    - You will **not** receive a ready-made AMSI bypass from this course.
    - Sourcing or developing one is **your responsibility**—this is part of the learning challenge.
- **Why This Matters:**
    - The upcoming exercises depend on being able to execute code without AMSI blocking it.
    - Without a working bypass, you will not be able to complete later tasks.

---

### **3. Expanding Your Learning – Beyond the Basic Bypass**

- **Advanced Exploration:**
    - Investigate patching other AMSI-related functions.
    - Understand **what** these functions do and **how** they influence detection and execution.
- **Hands-On Practice:**
    - Experiment in a safe lab environment.
    - Monitor changes in detection by testing your bypass with different payloads and scenarios.
- **Skill Goal:**
    - Develop the technical depth to modify or create new bypasses as defenses evolve.

---

### **4. The Challenge**

- **Your Task:**
    1. Create or source a **working AMSI bypass**.
    2. Test it to ensure it reliably disables AMSI scanning.
    3. Be ready to use it in the next lecture’s exercises.
- **Success Criteria:**
    - Your bypass should allow PowerShell or other AMSI-inspected scripts to run without triggering AMSI detections.

---

### **5. Final Note**

- This is **not** just about finding a one-time trick—it’s about:
    - Building adaptability.
    - Understanding how AMSI works.
    - Staying ahead of modern defensive measures.
- **Once you have your bypass ready**, you’re prepared to move forward in the course.
