<h2 id="1-understanding-the-shelf-life-of-evasion-techniques"><strong>1.
Understanding the Shelf Life of Evasion Techniques</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Key Point:</strong> Evasion techniques (such as AMSI
bypasses) do not work forever.</li>
<li><strong>Reason:</strong> Security tools like antivirus, EDR, and
AMSI itself are constantly updated.</li>
<li><strong>Impact:</strong> A bypass that is effective today may be
patched or signatured tomorrow.</li>
<li><strong>Takeaway:</strong> Success in this field requires
adaptability—constantly learning and modifying techniques as the
detection landscape changes.</li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="2-importance-of-having-a-working-amsi-bypass"><strong>2.
Importance of Having a Working AMSI Bypass</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Requirement:</strong> Before you proceed to the next course
stage, you <strong>must</strong> have a functional AMSI bypass.</li>
<li><strong>No Pre-Built Solution Provided:</strong>
<ul>
<li>You will <strong>not</strong> receive a ready-made AMSI bypass from
this course.</li>
<li>Sourcing or developing one is <strong>your
responsibility</strong>—this is part of the learning challenge.</li>
</ul></li>
<li><strong>Why This Matters:</strong>
<ul>
<li>The upcoming exercises depend on being able to execute code without
AMSI blocking it.</li>
<li>Without a working bypass, you will not be able to complete later
tasks.</li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="3-expanding-your-learning--beyond-the-basic-bypass"><strong>3.
Expanding Your Learning – Beyond the Basic Bypass</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Advanced Exploration:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Investigate patching other AMSI-related functions.</li>
<li>Understand <strong>what</strong> these functions do and
<strong>how</strong> they influence detection and execution.</li>
</ul></li>
<li><strong>Hands-On Practice:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Experiment in a safe lab environment.</li>
<li>Monitor changes in detection by testing your bypass with different
payloads and scenarios.</li>
</ul></li>
<li><strong>Skill Goal:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Develop the technical depth to modify or create new bypasses as
defenses evolve.</li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="4-the-challenge"><strong>4. The Challenge</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Your Task:</strong>
<ol type="1">
<li>Create or source a <strong>working AMSI bypass</strong>.</li>
<li>Test it to ensure it reliably disables AMSI scanning.</li>
<li>Be ready to use it in the next lecture’s exercises.</li>
</ol></li>
<li><strong>Success Criteria:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Your bypass should allow PowerShell or other AMSI-inspected scripts
to run without triggering AMSI detections.</li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="5-final-note"><strong>5. Final Note</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>This is <strong>not</strong> just about finding a one-time
trick—it’s about:

<li>Staying ahead of modern defensive measures.</li>
</ul></li>
<li><strong>Once you have your bypass ready</strong>, you’re prepared to

</ul>
