<h3 id="1-understanding-the-solo-purple-teaming-cycle"><strong>1.
Understanding the Solo Purple Teaming Cycle</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Definition</strong>: Solo purple teaming is an
<strong>iterative process</strong> designed to drive <strong>continuous
improvement</strong> in both offensive and defensive skills.</li>
<li><strong>Why Iterative?</strong>
<ul>
<li>After establishing your initial detections, the process restarts
with a new focus.</li>
<li>Each cycle pushes you to:
<ul>
<li>Develop <strong>bypasses</strong> through targeted research.</li>
<li>Identify <strong>detection gaps</strong>.</li>
<li>Improve <strong>adversarial thinking</strong>.</li>
</ul></li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="2-focus-of-this-phase--research-and-bypasses"><strong>2. Focus
of This Phase – Research and Bypasses</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Primary Goal</strong>:
<ul>
<li>Challenge your detection rules by <strong>researching attack
techniques</strong> that can bypass them.</li>
<li>Gain deeper <strong>technical insight</strong> into adversary
behavior.</li>
</ul></li>
<li><strong>Benefits</strong>:
<ul>
<li>Improves <strong>both attacker and defender
perspectives</strong>.</li>
<li>Maximizes your <strong>learning potential</strong>.</li>
<li>Leads to <strong>smarter detection strategies</strong>.</li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="3-role-of-threat-intelligence"><strong>3. Role of Threat
Intelligence</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Why Use Threat Intelligence (TI)?</strong>
<ul>
<li>Keeps your testing aligned with <strong>real-world, current
threats</strong>.</li>
<li>Helps you simulate the <strong>latest tactics, techniques, and
procedures (TTPs)</strong> used by adversaries.</li>
<li>Ensures your detections are <strong>operationally relevant</strong>
and not just theoretical.</li>
</ul></li>
<li><strong>If Working in an Organization</strong>:
<ul>
<li>Use <strong>tactical cyber threat intelligence</strong> to align
your purple team exercises with <strong>real risks</strong> to your
environment.</li>
<li>Prioritize <strong>improving defenses where they matter
most</strong>.</li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="4-impact-of-incorporating-ti"><strong>4. Impact of Incorporating
TI</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Sharpen your technical skills with <strong>live, relevant attack
scenarios</strong>.</li>
<li>Deepen your understanding of <strong>how threats manifest</strong>
in real systems.</li>
<li>Ensure your engagements <strong>bring measurable value</strong> to
your organization’s cybersecurity posture.</li>
</ul>
