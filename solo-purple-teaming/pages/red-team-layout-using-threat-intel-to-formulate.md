# Red Team Layout - Using Threat Intel to Formulate Next Attack Path

Owner: Mike Sterrett

### **1. Understanding the Solo Purple Teaming Cycle**

- **Definition**: Solo purple teaming is an **iterative process** designed to drive **continuous improvement** in both offensive and defensive skills.
- **Why Iterative?**
    - After establishing your initial detections, the process restarts with a new focus.
    - Each cycle pushes you to:
        - Develop **bypasses** through targeted research.
        - Identify **detection gaps**.
        - Improve **adversarial thinking**.

---

### **2. Focus of This Phase – Research and Bypasses**

- **Primary Goal**:
    - Challenge your detection rules by **researching attack techniques** that can bypass them.
    - Gain deeper **technical insight** into adversary behavior.
- **Benefits**:
    - Improves **both attacker and defender perspectives**.
    - Maximizes your **learning potential**.
    - Leads to **smarter detection strategies**.

---

### **3. Role of Threat Intelligence**

- **Why Use Threat Intelligence (TI)?**
    - Keeps your testing aligned with **real-world, current threats**.
    - Helps you simulate the **latest tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs)** used by adversaries.
    - Ensures your detections are **operationally relevant** and not just theoretical.
- **If Working in an Organization**:
    - Use **tactical cyber threat intelligence** to align your purple team exercises with **real risks** to your environment.
    - Prioritize **improving defenses where they matter most**.

---

### **4. Impact of Incorporating TI**

- Sharpen your technical skills with **live, relevant attack scenarios**.
- Deepen your understanding of **how threats manifest** in real systems.
- Ensure your engagements **bring measurable value** to your organization’s cybersecurity posture.
