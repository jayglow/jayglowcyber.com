# Fictional Scenarios

To make this course more engaging and enjoyable, we've created fictional scenarios that bring the concepts to life and give you a chance to apply what you learn in a fun, imaginative way. Instead of using fictional scenarios, threat intel, research, or historical events can be integrated into the solo purple teaming framework

# Offensive Security Terms

Reference: **https://redteam.guide/docs/definitions**

ASSUMED BREACH

- The Assumed Breach Model assumes a threat has some level of access to a target at the initiation of the engagement.

COMMAND AND CONTROL (C2)

- The influence an attacker has over a compromised computer system that they control.

OPSEC

- In terms of Red Teaming, it is understanding what actions defenders can observe and minimizes exposure.

SITUATIONAL AWARENESS

- Situational awareness is a step in a Red Team engagement used to gather as much information as needed on the targets and target environment. The information gathered is used to determine the next actions towards privilege escalation, lateral movement, or other steps. It is a crucial component to Red Teaming and should be performed to some level on all access targets.
