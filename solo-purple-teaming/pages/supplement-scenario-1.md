<h1 id="offensive-security-terms">Offensive Security Terms</h1>
<p>Reference: <strong><a
href="https://redteam.guide/docs/definitions">https://redteam.guide/docs/definitions</a></strong></p>
<p>ASSUMED BREACH</p>
<ul>
<li>The Assumed Breach Model assumes a threat has some level of access
to a target at the initiation of the engagement.</li>
</ul>
<p>COMMAND AND CONTROL (C2)</p>
<ul>
<li>The influence an attacker has over a compromised computer system
that they control.</li>
</ul>
<p>OPSEC</p>
<ul>
<li>In terms of Red Teaming, it is understanding what actions defenders
can observe and minimizes exposure.</li>
</ul>
<p>SITUATIONAL AWARENESS</p>
<ul>
<li>Situational awareness is a step in a Red Team engagement used to
gather as much information as needed on the targets and target
environment. The information gathered is used to determine the next
actions towards privilege escalation, lateral movement, or other steps.
It is a crucial component to Red Teaming and should be performed to some
level on all access targets.</li>
</ul>
