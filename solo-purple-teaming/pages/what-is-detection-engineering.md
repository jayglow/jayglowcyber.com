<h1 id="what-is-detection-engineering">What is Detection
Engineering?</h1>
<p>Owner: Mike Sterrett</p>
<h3 id="1-overview"><strong>1. Overview</strong></h3>
<p>In the upcoming series of lectures, we’ll explore <strong>detection
engineering concepts</strong> that will help you during the <strong>blue
team phases</strong> of solo purple teaming.</p>
<p>Detection engineering isn’t just about “watching the logs” — it’s
about <strong>designing, implementing, and refining</strong> threat
detection capabilities so they’re <strong>accurate, proactive, and
resilient</strong>.</p>
<hr />
<h3 id="2-what-is-detection-engineering"><strong>2. What is Detection
Engineering?</strong></h3>
<p><img src="What%20is%20Detection%20Engineering/image.png"
alt="image.png" /></p>
<p>Detection engineering:</p>
<ul>
<li>Goes <strong>beyond traditional monitoring</strong>.</li>
<li>Focuses on <strong>intentionally</strong> crafting rules, analytics,
and detection logic.</li>
<li>Ensures that detection works <strong>reliably</strong> within your
SIEM or detection platform.</li>
<li>Acts as a <strong>bridge</strong> between threat intelligence (what
we know about adversaries) and practical detection (what we can actually
catch).</li>
<li>Moves security from <strong>reactive</strong> (“we’ll respond when
it happens”) to <strong>proactive</strong> (“we’ll spot it before it
escalates”).</li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="3-why-detection-engineering-matters"><strong>3. Why Detection
Engineering Matters</strong></h3>
<p>Strong detection engineering:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Spots threats earlier</strong>, allowing faster
response.</li>
<li><strong>Reduces false positives</strong>, preventing alert
fatigue.</li>
<li><strong>Improves precision</strong> in alerts, so analysts can focus
on real threats.</li>
<li>Enables a <strong>threat-focused approach</strong>, making SOC
operations more effective.</li>
<li><strong>Increases resilience</strong> in security operations against
evolving threats.</li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h3 id="4-the-detection-engineering-process"><strong>4. The Detection
Engineering Process</strong></h3>
<p>Detection engineering is <strong>continuous</strong>. It
involves:</p>
<ol type="1">
<li><strong>Understanding threats</strong> – Learn how adversaries
operate and adapt.</li>
<li><strong>Building detection rules &amp; analytics</strong> –
Translate threat intel into detection logic.</li>
<li><strong>Testing detections</strong> – Use <strong>attack
simulations</strong> to confirm they work.</li>
<li><strong>Maintaining &amp; tuning</strong> – Adjust rules to reduce
false positives and adapt to new tactics.</li>
<li><strong>Iterating</strong> – Continuously improve as threats
evolve.</li>
</ol>
