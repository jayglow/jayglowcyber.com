# What is Detection Engineering?

Owner: Mike Sterrett

### **1. Overview**

In the upcoming series of lectures, we’ll explore **detection engineering concepts** that will help you during the **blue team phases** of solo purple teaming.

Detection engineering isn’t just about “watching the logs” — it’s about **designing, implementing, and refining** threat detection capabilities so they’re **accurate, proactive, and resilient**.

---

### **2. What is Detection Engineering?**

![image.png](What%20is%20Detection%20Engineering/image.png)

Detection engineering:

- Goes **beyond traditional monitoring**.
- Focuses on **intentionally** crafting rules, analytics, and detection logic.
- Ensures that detection works **reliably** within your SIEM or detection platform.
- Acts as a **bridge** between threat intelligence (what we know about adversaries) and practical detection (what we can actually catch).
- Moves security from **reactive** (“we’ll respond when it happens”) to **proactive** (“we’ll spot it before it escalates”).

---

### **3. Why Detection Engineering Matters**

Strong detection engineering:

- **Spots threats earlier**, allowing faster response.
- **Reduces false positives**, preventing alert fatigue.
- **Improves precision** in alerts, so analysts can focus on real threats.
- Enables a **threat-focused approach**, making SOC operations more effective.
- **Increases resilience** in security operations against evolving threats.

---

### **4. The Detection Engineering Process**

Detection engineering is **continuous**. It involves:

1. **Understanding threats** – Learn how adversaries operate and adapt.
2. **Building detection rules & analytics** – Translate threat intel into detection logic.
3. **Testing detections** – Use **attack simulations** to confirm they work.
4. **Maintaining & tuning** – Adjust rules to reduce false positives and adapt to new tactics.
5. **Iterating** – Continuously improve as threats evolve.
