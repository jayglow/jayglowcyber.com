<h1 id="factors-that-can-influence-c2-framework-choice">Factors That Can
Influence C2 Framework Choice</h1>
<h3 id="threat-intelligence">Threat Intelligence</h3>
<ul>
<li>Understanding your threat landscape helps determine which frameworks
are most likely to evade detection or mimic known threat actors.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="budget">Budget</h3>
<ul>
<li>Some frameworks require commercial licenses, while others are
open-source and free—your budget can narrow your options
significantly.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="knowledge">Knowledge</h3>
<ul>
<li>The technical proficiency of your red team plays a big role. Some
frameworks are plug-and-play, others require deep customization
skills.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="target-technologies">Target Technologies</h3>
<ul>
<li>The tech stack used by your target—Windows, Linux, macOS, on-prem or
cloud—can dictate compatibility and stealth of certain frameworks.</li>
</ul>
<h1 id="mythic-c2-framework">Mythic C2 Framework</h1>
<p><img src="Supplement%20Choosing%20C2%20Framework/image.png"
alt="image.png" /></p>
<p><strong>Mythic</strong> is a multiplayer command and control platform
for red teaming. Designed for
a <strong>plug-n-play</strong> architecture. Supports <strong>new
agents, communication channels, and modifications</strong> on the
fly.</p>
<p><strong>Main goals:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Enhance <strong>agent maintainability</strong>.</li>
<li>Enable <strong>customizations</strong>.</li>
<li>Provide <strong>robust data analytics</strong> for operations.</li>
<li>Improve <strong>operator quality of life</strong>.</li>
</ul>
