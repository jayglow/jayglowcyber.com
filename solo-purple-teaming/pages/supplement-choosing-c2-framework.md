# Supplement: Choosing C2 Framework

Owner: Josh

# Factors That Can Influence C2 Framework Choice

### Threat Intelligence

- Understanding your threat landscape helps determine which frameworks are most likely to evade detection or mimic known threat actors.

### Budget

- Some frameworks require commercial licenses, while others are open-source and free—your budget can narrow your options significantly.

### Knowledge

- The technical proficiency of your red team plays a big role. Some frameworks are plug-and-play, others require deep customization skills.

### Target Technologies

- The tech stack used by your target—Windows, Linux, macOS, on-prem or cloud—can dictate compatibility and stealth of certain frameworks.

# Mythic C2 Framework

![image.png](Supplement%20Choosing%20C2%20Framework/image.png)

**Mythic** is a multiplayer command and control platform for red teaming. Designed for a **plug-n-play** architecture. Supports **new agents, communication channels, and modifications** on the fly.

**Main goals:**

- Enhance **agent maintainability**.
- Enable **customizations**.
- Provide **robust data analytics** for operations.
- Improve **operator quality of life**.
